Provisional Display Bands: 85–100% Elite • 70–84% Strong • 55–69% Competitive • 40–54% Developing • <40% Needs Improvement. These bands represent descriptive historical indices, not future outcome probabilities.

All-Rounder Combined Weighting: When a player qualifies as an All-Rounder (≥30 pressure balls faced and ≥24 pressure balls bowled), the combined score applies 55% Batting Pressure % + 45% Bowling Pressure %. For specialist batters or bowlers, their primary discipline constitutes 100% of the rating.

Sample-Size Safeguards: A sample-size warning flag is automatically triggered when a batter has faced fewer than 20 pressure deliveries, or when a bowler has bowled fewer than 24 pressure balls. Scores in low-sample ranges must be interpreted provisionally.
